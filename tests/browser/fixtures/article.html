Local-first writing tools

The research team studied local-first writing tools in a carefully controlled experiment. The report describes how documents remain on the user's device while a compact language model helps with drafting, summarization, and translation. Participants reviewed every proposed edit before making any changes.

The second stage measured readability, response time, and energy use across several devices. Results varied by hardware and language, so the authors report the limitations alongside their observations. The study does not claim that a synthetic benchmark can replace evaluation with real readers and writers.

A practical implementation extracts only the article text, preserves paragraph order, and reports which sections have been processed. Optional narration uses a separately approved voice model, and stopping playback immediately discards queued audio. The original webpage is never rewritten by a summarization request.
